# Whiteboard photo to source

1. Snap a clear, well-lit photo of the equation.
2. Use equation OCR, a multimodal model, or Oleafly's image-to-LaTeX with your own key.
3. Compile immediately, then fix delimiter and index errors by eye.

A clear photo matters more than a fancy model. Crop tightly and avoid skew.
