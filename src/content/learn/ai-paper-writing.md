# Draft help that stays honest

## Good uses

- Outline and section restructuring suggestions
- Compressing related-work bullets
- Clarity edits on *your* draft

## Bad uses

- Invented citations (always check the `.bib` and the PDF)
- Unverified equations
- Submitting model prose you have not read

Keep the manuscript under Git. Treat AI like a fast intern: useful, and sometimes confidently wrong.
