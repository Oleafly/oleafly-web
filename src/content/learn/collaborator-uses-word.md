# Coauthors who only speak Word

## Survival guide

1. Keep `.tex` as canonical.
2. Export PDF for reading; export DOCX only when they must leave track-changes comments.
3. Schedule merge sessions: you apply accepted edits back into LaTeX.

Oleafly's Word import helps when *they* send the first draft as `.docx` and you take over typesetting.
